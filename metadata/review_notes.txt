The app asks for no permissions, so there is no usage-description string.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content shared with anyone else, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. "Delete all data" erases the reading stored on this device. It is not an account.

BassDaunito is a reading log that treats one open book as an expedition on a map. You record pages you have read, and once the page pool is large enough you sight the next stop and the marker moves along the line. It is for someone who wants reading progress as stops, drift, and a page pool, kept on this device.

No credentials or demo account are needed. On a first launch the app opens in dark mode as BassDaunito. The map may show dimmed with a spinner while saved reading loads. A three-step intro then replaces the map. Step "1" "of" "3" has "Skip", headline "Log pages on a map.", line "A book becomes a line of stops.", and "Next". Step "2" "of" "3" has "Sight the next stop." and "Twenty pages in the pool, then sight." Step "3" "of" "3" has "Keep the gap honest.", "Logging ahead of the marker shows drift.", and "Start". "Start" or "Skip" opens the map. A later launch skips the intro and keeps an open expedition. Home has no tab bar. Heading "MAP", large line "Log pages, then sight the next stop.", then "Shelf", "Session", "Expedition", "Pace", and "Settings". Each sheet has "Close". Empty map: "No expedition yet." and "Open a book to start." "Open a book" starts "The Surveyor's Coast". Status "Log pages, then sight." badge "SHORT". Stepper starts at 8, between 1 and 200, via "Fewer pages" and "More pages". "Log" three times (8, 8, 8) reaches 24. Status "Ready to sight." badge "READY". "Sight" spends twenty pages and moves the marker. Under twenty, badge "SHORT" again. While "Ready to sight.", "Log" does not respond until you sight. The title chip opens Shelf. "Drift" is "0°" until logged pages pull ahead. "Page pool" shows a number and "pages". Drag "AIM". Dragging does not log or sight. Shelf search: "Title or author", "Clear", and "Done". Books: "The Surveyor's Coast", "Night Latitude", "Paper Meridian", "Glass Bearing". A second book waits until this one is finished: "A book is already open. Finish it first." "Add a book" needs "Title" and whole "Pages" above zero. Session: "Log pages, then sight.", "PAGES", "Log", or "No pages logged yet." Expedition: "STOPS" as "Sighted", "Next", "Ahead", and "SIGHTS". Ready pool: "Sight the next stop". Finished: "This expedition is finished." Pace: "Reading pace", "Pages logged", "Sights taken", "Expeditions finished", "Continue the expedition". Settings: "ON THIS DEVICE", "Whole degrees", "Show intro again", "Delete all data" ("Delete every expedition on this device?", "Cancel"), "Contact", "Everything stays on this device."

Reading logs, books you add, and the intro flag stay on this device. Settings says "Everything stays on this device." "Contact" opens the support page.

Numbers and dates follow the device language and calendar. There is no separate region edition. The interface is dark on iPhone and iPad.

No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies, because this is a personal reading log and not a regulated service.

The app is a personal log only and gives no recommendations. It does not show health, medical, or product-health information, and it does not scan barcodes or QR codes.
